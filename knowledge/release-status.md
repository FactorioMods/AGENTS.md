## November 12 compatibility updates

Assembly Machines 1.1.6 introduced Small 3x3 and Large 5x5 families together, retaining existing entities and paid crafting jobs as legacy aliases. Its native headless checks covered saved-machine identity and paid crafting, ingredient/fluid capacities, robot-built rotated and mirrored blueprints, upgrades and deconstruction. Version 1.1.7 exposes the saved-alias layout selector. Native Factorio 2.1.24 checks confirmed selector visibility and matching results across all 251 evaluated prototype domains under both saved layouts; its control and prototype Lua retain the 1.1.6 runtime bytes.

Advanced Furnaces 1.1.4 recognizes the Loader Redux: Updated identity, preserving that provider's existing research unlocks while avoiding duplicate furnace-loader unlocks. Matt's Logistics 1.0.5 restores five optional Deadlock loader and beltbox tiers, preserving their existing names, recipes and artwork. It accepts original and compatible successor providers, honors independent loader and beltbox settings, and retains the Space Age turbo upgrade chain. Historical archives and source identities remain available in verified private backups.

The all-save compatibility checks use isolated copies, native headless servers and genuine offscreen Linux peers. Each published release's validation guide records its tested engine, features and graphical or connected-client coverage.
